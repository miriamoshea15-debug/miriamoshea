Biography

Miriam O'Shea

Born to a Moroccan father and Irish mother, Miriam O’Shea grew up between the Atlas Mountains in Morocco and Inishbofin Island in the west of Ireland. All of it shows up in her music: She weaves Sean Nós and Berber intonations with soul and jazz.

With her first EP scheduled for release in Winter 2027 following "Frozen", Miriam continues to craft a distinctive sound—fluid, ethereal, and deeply soulful.
