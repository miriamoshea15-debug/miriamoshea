Biography

Miriam O'Shea

Born to a Moroccan father and Irish mother, Miriam O’Shea grew up between Morocco and a remote island in the west of Ireland. All of it shows up in her music: She weaves Sean Nós and Berber intonations with soul and jazz.

With her first EP scheduled for release in early 2027 following "Frozen", Miriam continues to craft a distinctive sound—fluid, ethereal, and deeply soulful.
